About me

“Sofiko-ekimo” (Doctor Sofiko) — that's what my patients in Georgia call me

Education and experience

I grew up and trained as a doctor in St. Petersburg: Mechnikov North-Western State Medical University, then an internal medicine internship at Pavlov First St. Petersburg State Medical University. I worked as a gastroenterologist at three clinics across the city.

In 2022 I returned to my ancestral homeland, Georgia. I obtained a medical license and now see patients in Tbilisi and Batumi, and hold online consultations worldwide — in Russian, Georgian and English.

I believe a doctor's job is to explain, not to frighten. That's why I write a blog, give lectures for doctors and talk about digestion in a way that leaves you calm after the visit, not scared.
