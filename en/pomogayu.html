What I help with

from symptom to plan: what we treat, what we rule out, and what we leave alone

Digestion, nutrition and “confusing” test results

I look at the body as a whole — gastroenterologist, dietitian and internist in one. So you won't have to see three different doctors about one problem.

Heartburn and reflux

Burning, belching, a lump in your throat. Treatment follows modern protocols — not “just avoid acidic foods”.

Gastritis and H. pylori

Stomach pain and fullness; H. pylori eradication when indicated — without unnecessary courses of treatment.

Bloating and gas

We look for what's causing the gas instead of writing out a lifelong list of forbidden foods.

IBS, constipation and diarrhea

Irritable bowel syndrome is treatable — calmly, step by step, and without a “strict diet forever”.

Liver and gallbladder

Fatty liver disease, gallstones, “bile stagnation” and odd numbers in your test results — what matters and what doesn't.

Weight and eating habits

Weight loss without bingeing or guilt — a way of eating you can stick with for more than a month.

Pregnancy and breastfeeding

Heartburn, nausea, nutrition and supplements — only what's compatible with pregnancy and breastfeeding.

General health concerns

Fatigue, deficiencies, “confusing” test results — we make sense of them without scare tactics or unnecessary testing.
